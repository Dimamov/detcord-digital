GOAT COMMAND QUESTIONS

Ask clearly.
Stay in control.

What is GOAT Command?

GOAT Command is Detcord Digital’s approach to organizing digital work through plain-English requests. Tell us the outcome you need, then review the proposed work and approve the next step.

Can I ask for website and marketing updates in normal language?

Yes. Describe the change, such as updating business hours, adding a service or preparing a promotion. Include the relevant page, file or screenshot so we can understand the request.

Does GOAT Command replace every business tool?

The goal is to make requests and coordinated work easier across your agreed Detcord setup. Available tools, integrations and work depend on your scope. Talk to Detcord about the systems you already use.

Who approves changes made through GOAT Command?

You review and approve the proposed next step. The workflow is built around a clear rule: you ask, we do the work, you review and you approve.
