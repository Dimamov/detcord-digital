Website & SMS Terms

Updated October 3, 2026. These terms explain the public website, consultation requests and optional inquiry-related texting offered by Detcord Digital.

Consultation requests and paid services

A website inquiry requests a conversation; it does not purchase a service. Deliverables, fees, access, ownership, approvals and timelines for paid work are defined in a separate written proposal or service agreement.

Tools, examples and AI answers

Calculators and illustrative workflows support planning. They are not client results or guarantees. AI answers can contain mistakes. Confirm recommendations, scope and pricing with Detcord before acting on them. Do not submit sensitive information through the public form or chat.

Optional inquiry text messages

Detcord Digital uses the permission you provide to discuss your inquiry, consultation scheduling and related follow-up. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. SMS permission is optional and is not a condition of purchase; you can choose email instead. The inquiry form does not subscribe you to recurring promotional campaigns.

Reply STOP to opt out. Reply HELP for help. You can also ask to change your preferences by emailing info@detcorddigital.com. We will respect withdrawn permission. Messaging availability depends on your device, carrier and the communication tools in use.
