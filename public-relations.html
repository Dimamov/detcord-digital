PR starts with something worth saying.

Detcord develops the story before the distribution list. We look for real expertise, meaningful announcements, useful data, strong points of view and business milestones that can matter to a specific audience.

Positioning before outreach

Who should care? Why now? What is actually new, useful or interesting? Clear answers make pitches sharper and keep communications from sounding like promotional filler.

Thought leadership with substance

Executives and subject-matter experts often sit on years of useful knowledge. We help turn that expertise into commentary, contributed ideas, media-ready perspectives and owned content without manufacturing controversy.

Connect earned and owned media

PR should strengthen the website, social presence, search visibility and sales story. Relevant coverage and credible mentions become part of a larger trust system instead of disappearing after publication day.

Measure what can actually be measured

Coverage quality, referral activity, branded demand and downstream engagement provide more context than raw impression estimates. We avoid pretending earned media can always be reduced to a perfect direct-response equation.

Does a PR engagement guarantee coverage?

No. Editors and publishers decide what to cover. We develop a relevant story, supporting material and an outreach plan without promising placement.

What makes a business story useful to a publisher?

A clear audience benefit, a timely development and evidence that can be checked. We help identify the strongest supported angle before pitching.

How is public relations measured?

Relevant coverage, referral quality and useful brand demand; placements are not guaranteed.

Turn a credible story into usable media materials

Scope can include messaging, press releases, a media kit, journalist outreach and podcast pitching. The story, spokesperson approvals and target outlets are agreed first. Earned coverage remains an editorial decision; placements are not guaranteed.
