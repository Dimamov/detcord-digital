How we approach dental practice marketing

Capture existing demand

Explain the treatment, eligibility questions and appointment process. Campaigns for routine care and elective treatments should reflect different questions and scheduling needs.

Create and recover demand

An implant-treatment page should answer evaluation and next-step questions. A routine checkup page can prioritize location, availability and an easy scheduling request.

Close the measurement loop

Measure booked and attended appointments, treatment fit and available capacity. Keep patient information out of ordinary advertising and site-event payloads.

Booked and attended appointments
