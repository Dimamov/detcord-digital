Privacy Policy

Detcord Digital may collect information you voluntarily submit through forms or direct communication, plus standard website analytics once enabled. We use that information to respond to requests, provide services, operate the website and improve marketing performance. We do not publish personal information submitted through our contact forms.

Chat with The GOAT

When you use our AI assistant, your messages are sent to OpenAI to generate a response. The chat sends recent conversation history with each question. Detcord privately stores your questions, AI replies, timestamps and the page where your chat started in Cloudflare for 30 days after the last message, to review questions and improve our service. When email notifications are enabled, questions or transcripts are sent to Detcord through Resend after five minutes of inactivity. Email copies may remain in our inbox after the website copy expires. Do not include sensitive or personal information. Chat history is not saved in your browser storage. Avoid sharing sensitive information. Contact details submitted through our contact form are handled separately by our form provider. AI responses may contain mistakes; contact us to confirm project scope, pricing and availability.

This starter policy should be reviewed against the final analytics, advertising, CRM and cookie technologies enabled at launch.
