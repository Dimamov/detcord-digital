Privacy Policy

This notice explains how Detcord Digital uses information submitted through this website. Questions about your information can be sent to info@detcorddigital.com.

Chat with The GOAT

When you use our AI assistant, your messages are sent to OpenAI to generate a response. The chat sends recent conversation history with each question. Detcord privately stores your questions, AI replies, timestamps and the page where your chat started in Cloudflare for 30 days after the last message, to review questions and improve our service. When email notifications are enabled, questions or transcripts are sent to Detcord through Resend after five minutes of inactivity. Email copies may remain in our inbox after the website copy expires. Do not include sensitive or personal information. Chat history is not saved in your browser storage. Avoid sharing sensitive information. Contact details submitted through our contact form are handled separately by our form provider. AI responses may contain mistakes; contact us to confirm project scope, pricing and availability.

Contact requests

The contact form sends the details you submit to FormSubmit, our form provider, so we can receive and respond to your inquiry. The provider may use a verification challenge and process technical data to protect the form. Your message and contact preferences are used to discuss your request, and are not published on the website.

Website measurement

We use Google Analytics to understand website visits and interactions, such as service-link clicks, calculator use and opening chat. Google Analytics may use cookies and process technical information about your browser and device. Our custom events exclude names, email addresses, form entries and chat messages. Page URLs sent by our tracking code exclude query strings and fragments. Advertising personalization and Google signals are disabled. The form uses a temporary session marker to recognize a return from the form provider; it contains no contact information.

Service providers and your choices

Hosting, form and AI providers may process technical data while delivering their services. You may contact us by email instead of using the form or chat. To ask about access, correction or deletion of information you submitted, email info@detcorddigital.com. Include enough context to identify your request, without sending sensitive information.
