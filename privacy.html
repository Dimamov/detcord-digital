Privacy Policy

This notice explains how Detcord Digital uses information submitted through this website. Questions about your information can be sent to info@detcorddigital.com.

Client portal

Client accounts use a secure session cookie. We store client contact details, password hashes, reports, uploaded photos, invoice records and service agreements on Cloudflare. Electronic signing records the authenticated account, typed signature, consent, document hash, timestamp, and supporting IP/browser information. Executed agreements are retained as transaction records; contact us regarding access and retention. Each client can access their own records; Detcord administrators manage accounts and shared documents. Records remain until removed by Detcord or, for photos, by the client. Contact info@detcorddigital.com for access or deletion requests. Invoice payment links open Stripe, PayPal or Square checkout. Payment details are handled by that provider, and are not stored in this portal.

Chat with The GOAT

When you use our AI assistant, your messages are sent to OpenAI to generate a response. The chat sends recent conversation history with each question. Detcord privately stores your questions, AI replies, timestamps and the page where your chat started in Cloudflare for 30 days after the last message, to review questions and improve our service. When email notifications are enabled, questions or transcripts are sent to Detcord through Resend after five minutes of inactivity. Email copies may remain in our inbox after the website copy expires. Do not include sensitive or personal information. Chat history is not saved in your browser storage. Contact details submitted through our contact form are handled separately from chat. AI responses may contain mistakes; contact us to confirm project scope, pricing and availability.

Contact requests

The contact form is processed on Cloudflare and sends your inquiry to Detcord through Resend, our email delivery provider. We validate requests and temporarily retain a hashed network identifier and request count to limit spam. Your message and contact preferences are used to discuss your request, and are not published on the website.

Website measurement

We use Google Analytics to understand website visits and interactions, such as service-link clicks, calculator use and opening chat. Google Analytics may use cookies and process technical information about your browser and device. Our custom events exclude names, email addresses, form entries and chat messages. Page URLs sent by our tracking code exclude query strings and fragments. Advertising personalization and Google signals are disabled. The form uses a temporary session marker to recognize the confirmation page; it contains no contact information.

Service providers and your choices

Hosting, form and AI providers may process technical data while delivering their services. You may contact us by email instead of using the form or chat. To ask about access, correction or deletion of information you submitted, email info@detcorddigital.com. Include enough context to identify your request, without sending sensitive information.

Text-message choices

Text-message permission is optional and separate from other contact preferences. When you select Text and provide SMS consent, we use your number to respond to the inquiry, arrange a consultation and follow up about that request. This does not enroll you in a promotional subscription. Message frequency varies; message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to withdraw permission or HELP for help, or email info@detcorddigital.com.

We do not sell or share mobile numbers or SMS opt-in data and consent with third parties or affiliates for their marketing. Service providers may process that information only to support the communications you requested. Contact us to update your preferences.
