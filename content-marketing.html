Talk strategy ↗

Don't publish to feed a calendar.

Detcord content starts with a customer question, business objective or information gap. We create material that helps somebody understand a problem, compare an approach, evaluate a service or make a better decision.

Build the authority architecture

Core service pages establish what the business does. Supporting guides answer deeper questions. Industry content adds specific context. Original tools, frameworks and observations create material competitors cannot reproduce by changing a few nouns.

First-hand expertise beats commodity summaries.

We pull knowledge out of the people who actually do the work—sales conversations, customer objections, process details, real tradeoffs and lessons learned—then turn it into clear, structured content.

Designed for discovery and comprehension

Headings, internal links, concise answers, supporting detail, authorship and structured information help people and retrieval systems understand what a page is about and how it relates to the rest of the site.

Commercial content without the hard sell

The best content earns trust by being useful. Calls to action appear when they naturally help the reader take the next step.

What the work includes

  • Buyer-question and search-intent plan
  • Original articles, guides or landing-page content with a defined review process
  • Distribution, internal linking and content-performance measurement

What we need to start

First-hand expertise, approved claims and a subject-matter reviewer.

What we measure

Useful content engagement, relevant search demand and assisted qualified leads.

Scope and ownership

Before kickoff, confirm the deliverables, access, approval owner, costs, ownership and support responsibilities. Advertising budgets, third-party subscriptions and ongoing support are defined separately where relevant.

ILLUSTRATIVE SCENARIO · NOT A CLIENT RESULT

How this can work in practice

Interview the business about a recurring sales objection. Use its own explanation and a concrete example rather than rewriting a competitor’s article.

Do I need every service?

No. Start with the specific constraint in your customer journey. The right scope depends on the systems, evidence and capacity you already have.

Can you guarantee an outcome?

Results depend on demand, competition, implementation and your sales process. Agree the scope and measurement first; do not treat rankings, placements or revenue as guaranteed.
