The Detcord Lab · Field Model 001

Search is no longer ten blue links.

A practical model for earning visibility across traditional results, maps and AI-mediated discovery without chasing tricks.

The model

  • Accessible: search systems can crawl, render and understand the important content.
  • Relevant: pages directly satisfy real customer intents.
  • Specific: first-hand expertise and original detail beat generic summaries.
  • Connected: internal links create understandable topic relationships.
  • Identifiable: business, authorship and subject entities are clear and consistent.
  • Local when relevant: real-world business signals align with the market served.
  • Useful in passages: important answers are explicit, contextual and quotable.
  • Measurable: visibility is connected to qualified leads and revenue.

What this changes

We do not create separate “SEO content” and “AI content.” We build useful source material that is technically accessible, semantically clear and worthy of retrieval. The distribution surface may change; the underlying requirement to deserve attention does not.

GOAT NOTE: The target isn't an algorithm. The target is becoming the clearest credible source for the questions your buyers actually ask.
