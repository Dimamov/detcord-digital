Before buying more attention, inspect the attention you already earned.

Old leads, past customers, incomplete opportunities and lapsed buyers can represent a meaningful audience. Detcord segments that database around relationship and relevance before deciding what deserves a message.

Reactivation needs a reason.

“Just checking in” is rarely compelling. Service timing, seasonal need, new offerings, useful information, unfinished decisions and genuine customer milestones can create a better reason to reconnect.

Build referrals into the customer journey.

Referral requests work best after a real success moment. We identify those moments and make it easy for satisfied customers to introduce the business without turning the relationship into a constant solicitation.

Coordinate email, SMS and human follow-up.

Automation can reopen conversations, but high-intent responses should reach a person quickly. CRM status and contact history help keep the experience coherent.

Measure recovered opportunity.

Replies, appointments, opportunities, repeat purchases and referred customers tell a more useful story than send volume.

GOAT NOTE
Sometimes the cheapest new customer is an old customer who simply needed a reason to come back.

Which former customers should receive a reactivation offer?

We review the service history, relevance of the offer and contact permissions. A useful invitation should fit the recipient’s relationship with the business.

How do you avoid treating every returning customer as a campaign result?

We define a reporting window and distinguish observed responses from assumed causation. Existing demand and other marketing may also contribute.

How is referral & reactivation measured?

Incremental qualified opportunities, retained customers and recorded referral sources.

A focused campaign for customers you already know

Identify eligible former customers, define a relevant return offer or referral request, and agree the follow-up sequence. Compare accepted referrals and recovered customers with campaign costs. Use the email and SMS service for the broader ongoing lifecycle system.
