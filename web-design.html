Your website is part salesperson, part storefront, part search asset.

Detcord builds websites around the questions a prospect needs answered before taking the next step. Information architecture, copy, visual hierarchy, technical performance and conversion design are developed as one system.

Message before decoration

The first screen should quickly establish what you do, who it is for and why the visitor should care. Every major page gets a clear purpose and a deliberate next action.

SEO is built into the structure.

Semantic HTML, crawlable navigation, canonicalization, structured data, page speed, mobile usability and internal linking are considered during development rather than bolted on after launch.

Conversion without gimmicks

We reduce unnecessary decisions, place proof near moments of uncertainty, simplify forms and make calls to action obvious without turning the experience into a pop-up obstacle course.

Built to evolve

Analytics and conversion events create a feedback loop after launch. Pages can then improve based on actual visitor behavior, lead quality and business outcomes.

How this can work in practice

An ad promises roof inspections. The landing page should explain the inspection process, service coverage and what happens after the request, rather than opening with an unrelated slogan.

Do I need every service?

No. Start with the specific constraint in your customer journey. The right scope depends on the systems, evidence and capacity you already have.

Can you guarantee an outcome?

Results depend on demand, competition, implementation and your sales process. Agree the scope and measurement first; do not treat rankings, placements or revenue as guaranteed.
