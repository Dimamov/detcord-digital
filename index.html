BEFORE WE LIGHT THE FUSE

Good questions.
Straight answers.

What does Detcord Digital do?

Detcord Digital is a Michigan-based digital marketing agency. We help businesses with SEO, paid advertising, website design, CRM systems and AI automation. Visit our services page to explore the work.

Does Detcord Digital serve businesses in Michigan?

Yes. Detcord Digital is based in Michigan and serves Michigan businesses. We build the scope around your business, customers and goals. Explore the industries we work with.

How much does digital marketing with Detcord cost?

Pricing depends on the work your business needs. We discuss your goals, existing tools and priorities before proposing a custom scope. Book a free strategy call to discuss a starting point.

Do I need the whole system?

No. We start with the problem that matters most. That might be a website, search visibility, paid acquisition or follow-up. The strategy determines the scope.

Can you work with what I already have?

Yes. We assess your existing website, campaigns and tools first. Useful foundations stay; weak links get addressed.

How do you measure success?

We agree on the business objective and track the journey toward it: qualified leads, appointments, sales, revenue or retention. The right measure depends on the work.

What happens on the strategy call?

We talk through your business, your current marketing and where you want to go. Then we identify a sensible starting point and discuss whether Detcord is a fit.
