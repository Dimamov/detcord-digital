ILLUSTRATIVE WORKFLOW / PLUMBING BUSINESS

From inquiry to booked job.

A homeowner asks about a slow-draining kitchen sink. Here is one example of how a tailored lead-handling system could help. This is a demonstration, not a client case study or a promise of results.

01 / CAPTURE

Keep the inquiry in one place.

A website form creates a CRM record with the customer’s contact details, service request, preferred contact method and campaign source where available. The team receives a notification.

02 / ACKNOWLEDGE

Confirm the request was received.

An approved acknowledgment uses the customer’s selected channel. Example: “Thanks for contacting us about your kitchen drain. We have your request. Our team will contact you to confirm the details.”

The message confirms receipt; it does not confirm an appointment.

03 / QUALIFY & ROUTE

Find the right next step.

The workflow checks service area, requested work and urgency using rules agreed with the business.

  • Routine request: route to the scheduling team.
  • Urgent, unclear or outside scope: flag for a person to review.

The system does not diagnose the plumbing problem.

04 / CONFIRM & FOLLOW UP

Make the appointment real.

A staff member confirms availability and the job details, or the customer selects a valid slot from an integrated booking system. Once confirmed, the CRM stores the appointment and sends agreed reminders.

If the customer does not respond, the system creates a follow-up task or sends an approved message at the agreed time, respecting communication preferences and opt-outs.

05 / CLOSE THE LOOP

Track what happened.

The team marks the inquiry booked, completed, lost or needing more attention. Where available, the job value connects to the original campaign.

Useful measures: response time, qualified inquiries, booked jobs, completed jobs and attributed revenue. Missing data stays visible.

Your business sets the rules.

Service areas, qualification questions, staff assignments, integrations, message timing and reporting are customized after your free consultation. A law firm, mechanic or manufacturer would use a different workflow.
