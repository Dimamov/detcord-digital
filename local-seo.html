Talk strategy ↗

Local SEO is more than a map listing.

Customers move between map results, organic pages, reviews, photos, websites and phone calls while deciding who deserves their business. Detcord works on the system rather than treating each surface as an isolated checklist.

Google Business Profile

Categories, services, hours, business information, photos, review activity and other profile elements should accurately represent the real business. We optimize for clarity and completeness rather than gimmicks.

The website has to support the local story.

Service information, legitimate location relevance, internal linking, contact details and useful local context help customers—and search systems—understand where the business operates and what it actually does.

Reputation influences the decision.

Review quantity alone is not the strategy. Recency, specificity, response practices and the customer experience behind those reviews all matter. We build repeatable feedback and review-request workflows around real customer moments.

Citations and entity consistency

Important business information should agree across major profiles and directories. We prioritize accurate, relevant listings instead of manufacturing hundreds of low-value directory entries.

Measure calls, leads and customers.

Local visibility metrics help diagnose performance. Where tracking supports it, we connect calls, forms and appointments to qualified opportunities and revenue.

Bring your market into the lab.

We'll look at the actual search landscape, competitors, customer intent and conversion path before deciding what deserves attention.
