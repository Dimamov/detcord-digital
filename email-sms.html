GOAT // LIFECYCLE LABEngineering the right message for the right customer state at the right moment.

The money is often already in the database.

Businesses spend heavily acquiring attention and then underinvest in what happens after the first inquiry. Detcord designs email and SMS around lifecycle stages so leads are followed up, customers stay informed and past customers have a reason to return.

Different states need different messages.

A new lead, unresponsive prospect, booked appointment, active customer and dormant customer should not receive the same sequence. We segment communication around what is actually happening in the relationship.

Automation with a human exit.

Sequences can handle predictable follow-up while creating clear handoff points when a reply, high-intent action or exception needs a person.

SMS is powerful because it is personal.

We use text messaging selectively, with appropriate consent and opt-out handling. The goal is useful communication—not turning somebody's phone into another spam inbox.

Measure the business response.

Opens and clicks are diagnostic signals. Replies, appointments, reactivated customers, purchases and revenue are closer to the reason the program exists.

GOAT NOTE
Follow-up should feel timely, not robotic. Automation earns its place when it makes the customer experience better.
