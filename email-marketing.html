Your list is an asset you already own.

Search rankings change and ad costs rise. The people who already asked for your emails are an audience you do not rent. Detcord builds email marketing that keeps your business in front of them with messages they are glad to open, so past customers come back and new leads keep moving toward a decision.

Campaigns people actually want.

Newsletters, seasonal promotions, launches, event invitations and useful tips. Each campaign gets one clear purpose, copy written for your customers, a mobile-friendly design that matches your brand, and a send schedule that fits how often your customers actually want to hear from you.

Automated flows that work while you are busy.

Welcome series for new subscribers, lead nurture after an inquiry, post-purchase and post-service follow-up, review requests, and win-back emails for customers who have gone quiet. Flows are triggered by what a person did, so the right message goes out at the right moment without anyone pressing send.

Deliverability comes first.

A great email does nothing in a spam folder. We set up and check SPF, DKIM and DMARC on your sending domain, meet Gmail and Yahoo bulk sender rules including one-click unsubscribe, clean old and inactive contacts, and keep complaint rates low so inbox providers keep trusting your mail.

GOAT NOTE
Buying a list is like buying a fuse that is already wet. We grow your list the slow, legal way, and it lands in the inbox for it.

Segmentation instead of blasting.

A new subscriber, an active customer and someone who has not bought in two years should not get the same message. We segment by relationship, interest and behavior, and personalize where it actually helps the reader.

Measure the business response.

Opens are inflated by privacy features, so they are a weak signal. We report clicks, replies, booked appointments, purchases and revenue per send, alongside unsubscribes and complaints, and use A/B tests on subject lines, offers and timing to improve each send.

Do I need permission to email my customers?

For commercial email to people in the US, CAN-SPAM requires accurate sender details, an honest subject line, a physical mailing address and a working unsubscribe that is honored within 10 business days. It does not require opt-in, but permission-based lists perform better and protect deliverability, so we build list growth around clear sign-up choices.

Why are my emails going to spam?

Usually a mix of missing authentication (SPF, DKIM and DMARC), old or purchased contacts, low engagement and high complaint rates. Gmail and Yahoo also require bulk senders to offer one-click unsubscribe and keep spam complaints low. We check these before sending anything new.

Can you work with the email platform I already use?

Usually, yes. We work in common platforms such as Mailchimp, Klaviyo, Constant Contact and HubSpot, or in your CRM. If the current tool is holding you back, we explain why before recommending a change.

How is email marketing measured?

Opens are unreliable since privacy features started inflating them, so we treat them as a diagnostic signal only. We focus on clicks, replies, booked appointments, purchases and revenue per send, and we watch unsubscribes and complaints.

Email only, or email and text?

This service covers email on its own. If you also want text messages for reminders, missed-call follow-up or promotions, see Email & SMS Marketing, which adds SMS consent, opt-out handling and channel preferences. A focused win-back or referral push is covered under referral and customer reactivation.
