A dashboard should answer a business question.

Traffic, clicks and impressions explain activity. They do not tell you whether the activity created profitable customers. Detcord builds measurement around the path from acquisition source through lead quality, pipeline and revenue using the best data the business can reliably capture.

The measurement stack

  • GA4 and conversion-event architecture
  • Advertising pixels and platform events
  • Call tracking and source attribution
  • CRM source and lifecycle tracking
  • Qualified-lead and opportunity reporting
  • Sales and revenue feedback
  • Executive dashboards built around decisions

Attribution has limits. Good decisions still need context.

No attribution model perfectly reconstructs every human decision across devices, channels and offline conversations. We make those limitations visible instead of manufacturing precision, then use multiple signals to understand what is creating demand and customers.

Close the loop

When the CRM and sales process support it, downstream outcomes can be sent back into marketing analysis. That lets optimization move beyond form fills toward the leads and campaigns that actually become business.

Can tracking prove where every customer came from?

Not always. Consent choices, offline activity and disconnected systems create gaps. We explain those limits and agree on measures the available data can support.

Should names and messages go into analytics events?

No. Website-event measurement should describe actions rather than transmit form entries or chat content. We review the information included in each custom event.

How is analytics & attribution measured?

Qualified leads, opportunities, sales and contribution; avoid presenting uncertain attribution as exact.
