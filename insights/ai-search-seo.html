SEO for AI Search: Build for Understanding, Not Tricks

How strong technical SEO, original expertise and clear information architecture support discovery in AI-driven search.

AI-driven discovery changes how results can be synthesized, but it does not eliminate the fundamentals of making web information accessible, understandable and useful.

Make important information crawlable

Core services, expertise and answers should exist as accessible text with logical internal links. Do not hide the substance of the business inside images or interfaces search systems cannot reliably interpret.

Publish information worth citing

Generic summaries are easy to reproduce. First-hand experience, clear explanations, original frameworks, documented processes and real case studies are more differentiated.

Use structure to reduce ambiguity

Descriptive titles, headings, internal links and appropriate structured data help machines and people understand how pages relate to the organization and its services.

Measure emerging discovery separately

As search platforms expose more AI and multimodal reporting, track those surfaces without abandoning traditional lead and revenue measurement.
