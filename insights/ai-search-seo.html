SEO for AI Search: Build for Understanding, Not Tricks

By Detcord Digital · Michigan marketing field notes

How strong technical SEO, original expertise and clear information architecture support discovery in AI-driven search.

Start with the decision your buyer needs to make

A useful service page explains who the work is for, what it includes, what the buyer must supply and how progress will be measured. Those details help a person compare providers and give search systems concrete information to interpret.

Our approach is to audit the pages that support real buying decisions before adding more pages. Check whether the website says what the business actually does, whether important information is readable without opening a chat, and whether each page offers a useful next step.

A worked page improvement

ILLUSTRATIVE EXAMPLE · NOT A CLIENT RESULT

Before: “We deliver cutting-edge automation that transforms your business.”

After: “We connect website inquiries to your CRM, assign an owner, and trigger follow-up when an inquiry has no response. Before implementation, we map required fields, user permissions and failure alerts.”

The second version makes the service easier to evaluate. It describes an actual workflow without promising a ranking or a revenue increase.

Check technical eligibility before rewriting copy

  • Confirm important pages return a successful response and can be crawled.
  • Use consistent canonical URLs, descriptive internal links and an accurate sitemap.
  • Keep core information in accessible HTML and make the mobile experience usable.
  • Verify indexing and generative AI inclusion settings in Search Console.

What current Google guidance changes

Google says its existing SEO practices remain relevant to generative AI search. Its July 2026 guide also says there is no special AI schema, required content chunking or llms.txt requirement for Google Search. That supports spending effort on clearer information and a useful website before buying an AI-specific markup package.

Measure the business outcome

When reporting is available, review generative AI visibility separately from traditional search. Then connect inquiries to qualified opportunities and sales. A mention, impression or chat opening is not a sale. Use the search visibility model to organize the work and our AI search scope to see what implementation includes.
