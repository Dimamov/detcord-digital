Social media should have a job.

Posting because the calendar says Tuesday is not a strategy. Detcord defines what social is supposed to accomplish—recognition, trust, demand creation, customer education, community or remarketing support—then builds the content system around that job.

Strategy before volume

We identify the audience, platform behavior, brand voice, recurring content themes and the moments that can move somebody from passive awareness toward a conversation.

Content people recognize

A strong social presence develops visual and editorial patterns without becoming repetitive. Educational posts, proof, perspective, offers and personality work together so the business becomes familiar before the buyer needs it.

Organic and paid should talk to each other.

Organic content can reveal messages and creative themes worth amplifying. Paid distribution can extend strong ideas to specific audiences. We coordinate the two instead of operating separate content universes.

Measure beyond reactions.

Reach and engagement help explain content behavior. We also look for site visits, conversations, assisted conversions, lead quality and other downstream signals when they can be measured responsibly.
