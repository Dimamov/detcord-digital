How we approach chiropractic marketing

Capture existing demand

Describe the services the practice offers and how a first visit works. Keep claims supportable and let patients understand the next step without hype.

Create and recover demand

A patient who has never visited a chiropractor may need a clear first-visit explanation. Returning patients may simply need the right contact or scheduling path.

Close the measurement loop

Record inquiries, appointment requests and attended visits. Evaluate patient fit and scheduling capacity instead of treating every click as useful demand.

Decisions follow the evidence
