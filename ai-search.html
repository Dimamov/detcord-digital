Talk strategy ↗

AI search is not a separate internet.

Search experiences are changing, but the durable work remains familiar: publish genuinely useful information, make important pages accessible, establish who the business is, connect related topics and give systems clear evidence for important claims.

Entity clarity

Business name, services, people, locations and subject expertise should be represented consistently across the site and relevant external profiles. Appropriate structured data can reinforce those relationships; it cannot replace them.

Answers with substance

We structure important passages so the direct answer is clear, then support it with context, tradeoffs, examples and first-hand expertise. That serves human readers first while making retrieval easier.

Original source material

Experiments, calculators, frameworks, research, case evidence and experienced perspectives give other systems a reason to reference the business instead of another generic summary.

Technical access still matters.

Crawlability, internal links, canonicalization, performance and semantic page structure remain foundational. A brilliant answer hidden behind technical problems is still hard to discover.

Measure visibility without pretending the ecosystem is fully observable.

AI discovery reporting is evolving. We combine available referral, search, brand-demand and conversion signals while being explicit about what cannot yet be measured precisely.
