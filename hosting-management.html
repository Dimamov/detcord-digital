A website is operational infrastructure.

Campaigns, search visibility and customer journeys all depend on it being available and functioning correctly. Detcord manages the technical layer so preventable maintenance problems do not quietly become marketing problems.

Performance is ongoing work.

New scripts, images, integrations and content can gradually slow a site down. We monitor the experience and address avoidable performance debt rather than treating launch-day speed as permanent.

Maintenance and resilience

Depending on the platform, managed care can include SSL, backups, updates, uptime monitoring, deployment management and recovery planning. The goal is fewer surprises and a clear path when something breaks.

Marketing changes without chaos

Landing pages, tracking, content, forms and conversion paths evolve. Managed website care gives the business a controlled way to make those changes while protecting the underlying experience.

Ownership should stay clear.

Domains, DNS, analytics, hosting and critical accounts should be documented so the business understands what it owns and how the pieces connect.
