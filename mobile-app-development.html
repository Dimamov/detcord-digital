Build an app people have a reason to open.

A successful business app is not a website squeezed onto a phone. It is a focused product that makes a recurring customer or employee task faster, easier or more valuable. Detcord designs mobile experiences around that job first, then builds the technology around the experience.

What we can build

  • Customer portals and account experiences
  • Field-sales and field-service workflow apps
  • Scheduling, quoting and intake experiences
  • Loyalty, retention and customer communication tools
  • Internal operations and management applications
  • Progressive web apps when native distribution is unnecessary
  • CRM, database, payment, mapping and API integrations

Product strategy before pixels

We map the users, jobs, friction points, business rules and success metrics before deciding what belongs in version one. That keeps the product focused and prevents expensive features from being built simply because they sounded good in a meeting.

UX built for thumbs, interruptions and real life

Mobile users are often moving, distracted and operating on smaller screens. Navigation, tap targets, forms, authentication, camera use, notifications and offline or weak-network conditions all influence the design.

Connected to the rest of the business

The best app is rarely an island. We can connect the experience to CRM records, lead routing, customer data, automation and reporting so actions taken in the app become part of the same operating system.

Instrumented from launch

We define the actions that matter—activation, task completion, retention, lead creation, purchases or another business outcome—and build measurement around them. Downloads alone are not a product strategy.

Native app or progressive web app?

Native iOS and Android experiences can make sense when the product depends heavily on device capabilities, app-store distribution or persistent engagement. A progressive web app can be a better fit when fast deployment, broad accessibility and a single codebase matter more. We recommend the architecture based on the job, not the trend.

GOAT NOTE
The perfect app is not the one with the most features. It is the one that removes the most friction from something valuable people need to do repeatedly.

When does a business need an app?

When repeated behavior deserves a dedicated experience.

An app becomes compelling when customers or employees repeatedly perform tasks that benefit from a persistent mobile-first experience—especially field workflows, portals, scheduling, specialized operational tools, loyalty or high-frequency customer interactions.
