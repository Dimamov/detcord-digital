Buy demand. Don't rent vanity metrics.

Detcord builds paid-search programs around the economics of a customer: what qualified demand is worth, what the sales team can close and what the business can responsibly spend to acquire it.

Campaign architecture follows intent

High-intent searches, brand demand, competitor context, remarketing and broader discovery behave differently. We separate them where useful so budget, messaging and measurement can reflect the job each campaign is doing.

The landing page is part of the ad system.

Keyword, ad promise, page headline, proof, offer and conversion path should feel like one conversation. We treat landing-page friction and message mismatch as campaign problems—not somebody else's department.

Lead quality closes the loop.

Conversion tracking starts the measurement process. Call quality, qualified opportunities, booked appointments, closed sales and attributable revenue tell us whether the traffic was valuable.

Continuous experimentation

Search terms, negatives, match behavior, creative, offers, audiences, geography, schedules and landing experiences are reviewed as evidence accumulates. Automation is useful when it is fed useful signals.
